NOOR QUR'AN

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Summary: Noor Qur'an does not require an account, does not show ads, and does not collect, sell, or share your personal data. Everything you do in the app stays on your device.

1. Information We Collect

We collect no personal information. The app has no sign-up, no login, and no analytics or advertising SDKs.

  • Qur'an, translations and tafsir are bundled inside the app and read offline. No request is sent anywhere.
  • Duas, Asmaul Husna and the reciter catalogue are also bundled offline.
  • Bookmarks, last-read position, tasbih count and preferences are stored only in your device's local storage and never leave it.

2. Location Data

Location is used only to calculate the Qiblah direction and prayer times. It is:

  • requested only after you tap the location button;
  • processed entirely on your device;
  • never transmitted to us or any third party;
  • never stored on any server (only cached locally so the app remembers your last position).

You may deny location permission and instead set your city manually. The rest of the app works fully without it.

3. Audio Streaming

When you play murottal, the app streams audio directly from public Qur'an audio servers. As with any internet request, those servers may see your IP address and a standard user-agent. We do not control those servers and receive no data about you from them.

  • mp3quran.net — per-surah recitations and live radio
  • cdn.islamic.network — verse-by-verse recitations
  • youtube.com / youtu.be — YouTube recitations (subject to Google's Privacy Policy)

4. Permissions

  • Internet — streaming murottal audio.
  • Location — Qiblah direction and prayer times (optional).
  • Vibration — haptic feedback in the digital tasbih.
  • Foreground service / wake lock — keep audio playing with a media notification.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

6. Data Deletion

Because we store nothing on our servers, there is nothing for us to delete. To remove all app data, clear the app's storage in your device settings or uninstall the app.

7. Open Source & Data Sources

The Qur'anic text, translations, tafsir and duas come from respected public sources and are reproduced faithfully and unmodified:

  • Arabic text — Tanzil / Al-Qur'an Cloud (Uthmani)
  • Indonesian translation — Kemenag
  • English translations — Saheeh International, A. J. Arberry
  • Tafsir — Jalalayn
  • Duas — Hisnul Muslim
  • Prayer time calculation — adhan (Batoul Apps)

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
